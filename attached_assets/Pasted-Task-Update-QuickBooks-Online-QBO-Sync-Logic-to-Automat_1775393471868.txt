Task: Update QuickBooks Online (QBO) Sync Logic to Automate Stripe Fee Reconciliation

Objective:
Modify the current 2-way QuickBooks sync so that when a Stripe payment is processed, the system automatically accounts for Stripe fees. This will ensure the QBO bank deposit matches the actual net amount received in the bank feed.

Requirements:

1. Payment Recording (Undeposited Funds):

When a Stripe payment is successful, do not post the payment directly to the bank account.

Instead, record the Payment entity in QBO for the Gross Amount (the full invoice total) and set the DepositToAccountRef to the "Undeposited Funds" account.

2. Fee Retrieval & Deposit Creation:

Retrieve the fee amount from the Stripe balance_transaction object associated with the payment.

Create a Deposit entity in QBO that combines two lines:

Line 1 (Linked Payment): Link the Payment created in Step 1 (the Gross Amount).

Line 2 (The Fee): Add a new line to the deposit with a negative value equal to the Stripe fee.

Account Mapping: Map this negative line to the QBO expense account labeled "Bank Charges" or "Merchant Service Fees."

3. Reconciliation Logic:

Ensure the final TotalAmt of the QBO Deposit matches the net amount from Stripe (Gross - Fee).

Include error handling to verify that the Deposit is only created after the Payment has been successfully confirmed in QBO.

Technical Context:

Use the existing QBO OAuth2 tokens and client configuration.

Update the webhook handler/sync service responsible for processing payment_intent.succeeded or your internal "Invoice Paid" event.

A few tips for the implementation:
The "Undeposited Funds" ID: QBO account IDs vary by user. You might want to have your code look up the account by AccountType ("Asset") and AccountSubType ("UndepositedFunds") rather than hardcoding an ID.

The Expense Account: Similarly, ensure your SaaS allows the user to select which expense account they want the fees to hit (usually "Merchant Service Fees") during the setup of your integration.
